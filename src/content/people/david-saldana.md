David Saldaña leads SwarmsLab in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Lehigh University. His research focuses on flying modular robots, cooperative multi-robot systems, and resilient robot swarms.

Before joining Lehigh, he worked as a postdoctoral researcher in the [GRASP Laboratory](https://www.grasp.upenn.edu/) at the University of Pennsylvania with Prof. Vijay Kumar. He completed his PhD at the Federal University of Minas Gerais, Brazil, advised by Prof. Mario Fernando Campos and Prof. Renato Assunção in the Laboratory of Computer Vision and Robotics (VeRLab).

## Education and experience

- **2025–present:** Associate Professor, Computer Science & Engineering, Lehigh University.
- **2019–2025:** Assistant Professor, Computer Science & Engineering, Lehigh University.
- **2026–present:** Courtesy appointment, [Mechanical Engineering & Mechanics](https://engineering.lehigh.edu/meche), Lehigh University.
- **2017–2019:** Postdoctoral researcher, GRASP Laboratory, University of Pennsylvania.
- **2015–2017:** Visiting research scholar, GRASP Laboratory, University of Pennsylvania.
- **2013–2017:** PhD in Computer Science, Federal University of Minas Gerais, Brazil.
- **2011–2012:** MSc in Engineering — Systems Engineering, Universidad Nacional de Colombia, Medellín.
- **2005–2010:** BSc in Engineering of Systems and Informatics, Universidad Nacional de Colombia, Medellín.
